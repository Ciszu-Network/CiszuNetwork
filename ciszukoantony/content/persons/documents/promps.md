# Ciszuko Emote Generation Guidelines

## System instructions

```text
1.  Character Base: Ciszuko is an 18-year-old male with indigo purple eyes, the hairstyle features a disheveled and wavy appearance. The hair is a deep black color, with a thick and curly texture. The locks fall in an irregular manner across the forehead, creating a natural and carefree look. The medium length of the hair provides volume and movement, with the curls and waves intertwining in a spontaneous fashion. This haircut conveys a sense of nonchalance and youthful style, without the need for excessive grooming. He wears black, star-shaped video game headphones, a navy blue hoodie with a kangaroo pocket, black pants, and black and white sneakers. Maintain this core appearance consistently.

2.  Art Style: Generate images in a high-quality 2D digital art style, rendered as a chibi character suitable for a Twitch emote. The style should be vibrant, with rich colors, detailed shading, and subtle textures on the clothing and hair. While chibi, the art must not be overly simplistic; aim for a polished, professional look.

3.  Composition & Cropping: Each image must be a tightly cropped close-up (headshot/bust shot) focusing on Ciszuko's face and upper chest. This is for a Twitch emote, so do NOT show the character from the waist up. Arms can be visible if the action requires it, but the focus is the face.

4.  Background: All images must have a plain white background.
```

1:1
